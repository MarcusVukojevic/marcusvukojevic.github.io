      <div>
        <h2>SPaRKLE</h2>
        <p class="mv-case-study__lead">A compact gamma-ray payload selected for ESA’s Space Rider programme.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <p>SPaRKLE was selected through the ESA Academy Experiments Programme for in-orbit testing. The team also secured a competitive €40K Fondazione VRT grant for key hardware.</p>
        <p>As software and AI work-package lead, I develop methods to cluster gamma-ray events and turn detector signals into meaningful structure.</p>

        <h2>Lidy</h2>
        <p class="mv-case-study__lead">A multilingual AI guest assistant for hotels, apartments, and B&amp;Bs.</p>
      </div>
